# Fish Rescue · 接鱼回缸

Free single-file HTML5 arcade game. A fisherman on a sunset pier tosses fish
into the air every 1.2 seconds — move your bowl, catch them before they hit
the sea. Goldfish +1, koi +3, rare golden dragonfish +10, junk (boot/can) -2.
Chain catches for a x2-x5 combo (3 misses break it), a crow snatches fish
mid-air, and the tide doubles the throws in the last 20 seconds. One HTML
file, zero external assets.

**Play:** https://shuangyueliao.github.io/fish-rescue/

- Touch / mouse / arrow keys, canvas auto-fit, 60-second rounds
- Procedural graphics (sunset sea, pier, five hand-coded sprite kinds) and
  WebAudio-synthesized sounds (splash, ding, crow caw) — no dependencies
- Best score saved locally, mute toggle, no tracking, no external links
