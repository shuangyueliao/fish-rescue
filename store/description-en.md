# Fish Rescue — Store Submission Materials

## Short description (CrazyGames / GameDistribution / itch.io)

Catch falling fish in your bowl! A fisherman tosses goldfish, koi and rare
golden dragonfish from a sunset pier - dodge junk, outsmart the crow, ride
the tide. 60 seconds of relaxing arcade fun.

(194 characters, ≤200)

## Details

- Genre: Arcade / Casual / Relaxing
- Controls: Mouse / Touch (drag to move the bowl). Keyboard: ←/→ move.
- Scoring: Goldfish +1, Koi +3, Golden Dragonfish +10 (rare, sparkles);
  Boot/Tin can junk -2 (never costs a life). Combo multiplier x2-x5 for
  chained catches; missing 3 fish breaks the chain.
- Events: A crow flies across and snatches an airborne fish (steals its
  value). Tide rises in the final 20 seconds and doubles the throw rate.
- Features: 60-second rounds, best score saved locally (try/catch guarded),
  mute toggle, splash/particle effects, procedural sunset sea, pier and five
  hand-coded sprite kinds, WebAudio-synthesized sounds (plop, ding, caw).
- Tech: Vanilla JavaScript, Canvas, WebAudio. Single-file HTML5, no
  frameworks, no external assets, no tracking, works offline once loaded.
